What the assistant handles

Answers every call
Nights, weekends, and holidays. Callers get a calm, clear voice instead of a voicemail box.
Books the job
It takes the name, address, and problem, then books an arrival window. After-hours fees are stated before anything is booked.
Gets emergencies to a person
A gas smell or CO alarm gets safety instructions and goes straight to your on-call tech. So does no heat with someone vulnerable at home.
Sends you the details
Every call ends with an email summary. Urgent calls are flagged so they don't wait until morning.

How setup works

  1. We talk through your calls

  2. I build it and you test it

  3. Forward your after-hours calls

    You keep your phone number. Calls forward to the assistant when the office closes.
